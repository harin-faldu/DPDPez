Delete my account

Section 12(3) of the Act.

What this removes

  • The account row itself, including the contact address, name, telephone number and date of birth.
  • Seller verification records, orders, complaint tickets, guardian confirmations, nominees and the consent history linked to the account.
  • Any queued message that has not gone out.

What it does not remove

The daily analytics counters. They are keyed by a value that cannot be reversed into an account, so this step does not delete them. That is a known limitation of this fixture and it is written up in the fixture notes rather than hidden here.
