Complaint handling and escalation

Section 13 of the Act. Anyone can read this procedure. Filing against an account needs a sign in, so that we answer the right person.

How it works

  1. Tell us, using the form below or by writing to the officer whose address is at the bottom of every page.
  2. We record it with a deadline of {{ response_days }} days written onto the ticket itself, so the clock is a stored fact rather than a promise.
  3. We answer within that window and tell you what we did.
  4. If you are not satisfied, or we miss the window, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India under section 13(3). You do not need our permission and you do not have to tell us first that you intend to.
