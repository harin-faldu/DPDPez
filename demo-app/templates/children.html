Notice for accounts held by someone under eighteen

Version {{ policy_version }}. Section 9 of the Act.

How we know

Every account gives a date of birth when it is created. We work the age out from it rather than asking someone to tick a box claiming to be an adult, and a date we cannot read is treated as a child rather than as an adult.

What changes for a child's account

  • A parent or lawful guardian has to confirm the account through a challenge sent to their own contact address. Until they do, the account can sign in and do nothing else.
  • Analytics counting is refused outright for the account, whatever any consent record says, because section 9(3) prohibits tracking a child rather than merely requiring consent for it.
  • No promotional email is sent to the account, and it is never included in a campaign audience.

If a guardian wants something removed

Write to the Grievance Officer, whose address is at the bottom of every page, or use the erasure page from the account itself. We answer within thirty days.
