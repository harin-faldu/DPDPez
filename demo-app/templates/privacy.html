3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.
  • We do not run third party analytics, advertising pixels or tag managers. The analytics counters are computed on this server and no outside company sees them.
  • We do not profile you to make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not track anyone whose account is flagged as belonging to a person under eighteen, and we do not direct advertising at them. See the notice for under 18 accounts.

4. Consent, and taking it back

Two purposes run on consent: usage analytics and offers by email. Both start switched off, both are asked for separately, and neither is a condition of using the site or of buying anything. Withdrawing is one click on the manage preferences page, takes effect at once, and is exactly as easy as giving consent was, as section 6(4) requires. We keep a record of every consent and withdrawal, including which version of this notice it was given against, so you can ask us to show you what you agreed to and when.

8. How we protect it

Personal data columns are encrypted at rest with a per column cipher, so a copied database file is unreadable without the key. Credentials are put through a slow key derivation function and can never be read back. Every page that can reach personal data is behind a sign in, every read and write is written to an audit trail, and the audit entries hold a reference to the row rather than a second copy of its contents. The full description is on the security page.

9. How long we keep it

Each row gets an end date when it is written, taken from the retention period of the purpose it was collected for, and a scheduled job deletes rows once that date passes. The periods are in the table in section 2.
