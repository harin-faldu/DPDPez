What we collect, and why, before you type anything

The full text is on the privacy notice, linked from the top of every page rather than only from the bottom. The short version is below.

  • Account creation and sign in. Contact address, name, telephone number and date of birth. Kept for five years after the account closes.
  • Order processing and delivery. Delivery address and order history. Kept for eight years, because tax law requires it.
  • Seller verification. Permanent account number and the last four digits of an Aadhaar number, for sellers only, because a payout cannot be released without it.
  • Complaint handling. Your correspondence, kept for three years.
  • Usage analytics. Optional, off unless you switch it on, counted on this server with no outside company involved.
  • Offers by email. Optional, off unless you switch it on.
