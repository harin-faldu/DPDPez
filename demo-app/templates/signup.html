What this form collects, and why

  • Contact address. To sign you in and to send order updates.
  • Name. To put on the delivery label.
  • Telephone number. Optional, for the courier to call you.
  • Date of birth. To work out whether the account belongs to a person under eighteen. If it does, a parent or guardian has to confirm it, and we do not count analytics for it or send it offers.
  • Passphrase. Put through a slow one way function. We cannot read it.
